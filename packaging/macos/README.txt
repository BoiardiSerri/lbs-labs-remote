LBS Labs Remote for Mac - how to install
=======================================

1. Drag the "LBS Labs Remote" icon onto the "Applications" folder next to it.

2. Open the Applications folder and double-click LBS Labs Remote.
   The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the app (it is not
   distributed through the App Store). This is expected:
     - click "Done";
     - open  > System Settings > Privacy & Security;
     - scroll to the bottom: next to "LBS Labs Remote" click "Open Anyway"
       and confirm with your Mac password.

3. On the Mac THAT WILL BE CONTROLLED remotely, two permissions are needed
   (only once). macOS asks for them on first launch; if you skipped them,
   click the LBS Labs Remote icon in the menu bar (the small screen at the
   top right) and choose "Grant ...":
     - Screen Recording  -> turn on the switch next to LBS Labs Remote
     - Accessibility     -> turn on the switch next to LBS Labs Remote
   Then choose "Quit" from the icon menu and open LBS Labs Remote again.

4. To start it automatically: menu bar icon > check "Start at login".

To control another computer: open LBS Labs Remote, pick the computer from
the list (or type its IP address) and press Enter.
If the window is closed: menu bar icon > "Connect to a remote computer…".
